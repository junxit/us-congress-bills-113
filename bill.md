# S. 1963

> To repeal section 403 of the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2013.

## § 1. Repeal of certain reductions made by the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2013

Section 403 of the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2013 (Public Law 113–67) is repealed as of the date of the enactment of such Act.
