# H.R. 1545

> To amend the definition of State in certain Federal agricultural laws to include the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.

## § 1. Inclusion of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands as a state under certain federal agricultural laws

- **(a)** *Amendment to the McIntire-Stennis Cooperative Forestry Act* Section 8 of Public Law 87–788 (16 U.S.C. 582a–7; commonly known as the McIntire-Stennis Cooperative Forestry Act) is amended by striking “and Guam.” and inserting “Guam, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.”.
- **(b)** *Amendment to the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946* Section 207 of the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1626) is amended by striking “and Guam.” and inserting “, Guam, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.”.
