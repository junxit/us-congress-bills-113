# H.R. 4658

> To amend title XI of the Social Security Act to eliminate civil money penalties for inducements to physicians to limit services that are not medically necessary, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Eliminating civil money penalties for inducements to physicians to limit services that are not medically necessary

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1128A(b)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1320a–7a(b)(1)) is amended by inserting “medically necessary” after “reduce or limit”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to payments made on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
