# S. 2574

> To make the United States Preventive Services Task Force subject to the Federal Advisory Committee Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Healthy Families Act of 2014”.

## § 2. United States Preventive Services Task Force

Section 915(a) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 299b–4(a)) is amended by striking paragraph (5) and inserting the following:

> - **(5)** *Applicability of FACA* The Federal Advisory Committee Act (5 U.S.C. App.) shall apply to the Task Force.
