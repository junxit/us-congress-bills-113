# H.R. 1822

> To extend the authorization period for certain uses of funds from the San Gabriel Basin Restoration Fund.

## § 1. San Gabriel Basin restoration

- **(a)** *Extension* Section 110(a)(3)(A)(ii) of division B of the Miscellaneous Appropriations Act, 2001 (as enacted into law by section 1(a)(4) of Public Law 106–554) is amended by striking “10” and inserting “15”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to any project constructed under Section 110 of such Act before, on, or after the date of enactment of this Act.
