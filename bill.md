# S. 2506

> To award grants to States to support efforts at institutions of higher education to increase degree attainment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Correctly Recognizing Educational Achievements To Empower Graduates Act” or the “CREATE Graduates Act”.
