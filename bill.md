# S. 1339

> To reauthorize the Ohio & Erie Canal National Heritage Canalway.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Ohio & Erie Canal National Heritage Canalway

Section 809 of the Ohio & Erie Canal National Heritage Corridor Act of 1996 (16 U.S.C. 461 note; Public Law 104–333; 122 Stat. 826; 127 Stat. 420) is amended by striking “2013” and inserting “2022”.
