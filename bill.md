# H. RES. 225

> Raising a question of the privileges of the House.

That the House of Representatives shall not consider H.R. 3, the “Northern Route Approval Act” because: (1) it violates rule XXI of the House, and (2) it affects the dignity and integrity of the proceedings of the House since it is unconstitutional.
