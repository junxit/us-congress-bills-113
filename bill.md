# H.R. 4548

> To direct the Secretary of Labor to include programs that teach technology literacy in any job training program for ex-offenders offered under the Workforce Investment Act of 1998.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Re-Integration of Ex-Offenders Through Technology Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of technology literacy in ex-offender job training programs

The Secretary of Labor shall include programs that teach technology literacy in any job training program for ex-offenders offered under the Workforce Investment Act of 1998 (29 U.S.C. 2801 et seq.).
