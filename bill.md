# H.R. 5115

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to improve the beneficiary travel program of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Veteran Access to Care Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of beneficiary travel program

Section 111(b)(1)(B) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “rated at 30 percent or more”.
