# S. 1753

> To extend Government liability, subject to appropriation, for certain third-party claims arising from commercial space launches.

## § 1. Launch liability extension

Section 50915(f) of title 51, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2013” and inserting “December 31, 2016”.
