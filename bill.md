# S. 1774

> To reauthorize the Undetectable Firearms Act of 1988 for 1 year.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Undetectable Firearms Reauthorization Act of 2013”.

## § 2. Extension of sunset provision

Section 2(f)(2) of the Undetectable Firearms Act of 1988 (Public Law 100–649; 18 U.S.C. 922 note) is amended in the matter preceding subparagraph (A) by striking “25” and inserting “26”.
