# H.R. 5155

> To prohibit the National Endowment for the Humanities to provide funds to carry out the Popular Romance Project or any similar project relating to love or romance.

## § 1. Prohibition

The National Endowment for the Humanities may not provide funds to carry out the Popular Romance Project or any similar project relating to love or romance.
