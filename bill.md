# S. 2198

> To direct the Secretary of the Interior, the Secretary of Commerce, and the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to take actions to provide additional water supplies and disaster assistance to the State of California and other Western States due to drought, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Emergency Drought Relief Act of 2014”.
