# H.R. 2533

> To impose a moratorium on conferences held by the Internal Revenue Service.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Playing on Citizen’s Cash Act”.

## § 2. Moratorium on IRS conferences

The Internal Revenue Service shall not hold any conference until—

- **(1)** the Treasury Inspector General for Tax Administration submits a report to Congress—
  - **(A)** certifying that the Internal Revenue Service has implemented all of the recommendations set out in such Inspector General’s report titled Review of the August 2010 Small Business/Self-Employed Division’s Conference in Anaheim, California, and
  - **(B)** describing such implementation, and
- **(2)** the Internal Revenue Service is authorized by law (enacted after the date of the enactment of this Act) to resume holding conferences.
