# H.R. 4744

> To prohibit funding of the Rural Utilities Service High Energy Cost Grant Program.

## § 1. Prohibition on funding of the Rural Utilities Service High Energy Cost Grant Program

No funds available to the Department of Agriculture or any other department or agency may be used to provide funding directly or indirectly for grants under section 19(a)(1) of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936.
