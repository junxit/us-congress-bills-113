# H.R. 3860

> To revise the formula for allocating funding to States under the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Low Income Heating Improvement Act”.

## § 2. LIHEAP State allocation formula

Section 2604(a)(2) of the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 (42 U.S.C. 8623(a)(2)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(2)** For purposes of paragraph (1), a State's allotment percentage is the percentage which that State would have received under this section as enacted in Public Law 97–35.
