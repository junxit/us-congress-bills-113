# H.R. 681

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide that Washington’s Birthday be observed on February 22, rather than the third Monday in February, of each year.

## § 1. Change in date for observance of Washington’s birthday

-  Section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking the item relating to Washington’s Birthday and inserting the following:
  -  “Washington’s Birthday, February 22.”.
