# S. RES. 579

> Relative to the death of Howard O. Greene, Jr., former Sergeant at Arms of the United States Senate.

## Preamble

- Whereas Howard O. Greene, Jr., began his service to the United States Senate as a Doorkeeper in January 1968;
- Whereas Howard O. Greene, Jr., served the United States Senate as Republican cloakroom assistant, Assistant Secretary for the Minority, Secretary for the Minority, and Secretary for the Majority;
- Whereas Howard O. Greene, Jr., was elected as Senate Sergeant at Arms during the 104th Congress; and
- Whereas Howard O. Greene, Jr.'s more than 28 years of service was characterized by a deep and abiding respect for the institution and customs of the Senate: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate has heard with profound sorrow and deep regret the announcement of the death of Howard O. Greene, Jr.
