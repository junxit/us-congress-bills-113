# S. 2943

> To amend Public Law 110–299 to extend the time period during which permits are not required for certain discharges incidental to the normal operation of vessels.

## § 1. Discharges incidental to normal operation of vessels

Section 2(a) of Public Law 110–299 (33 U.S.C. 1342 note) is amended by striking “December 18, 2014” and inserting “December 18, 2015”.
