# S. 2418

> To amend title 11 and title 29, United States Code, to increase the amount of unsecured claims for salaries and wages given priority in bankruptcy, to provide for payments to retirees to compensate for lost health insurance benefits resulting from the bankruptcy of their former employer, to protect the health benefits of employees and retirees, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Bankruptcy Fairness and Employee Benefits Protection Act of 2014”.
