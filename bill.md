# H.R. 4928

> To prohibit certain closures of Senior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps programs of the Army.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Readying Our Troops for Combat Act of 2014” or the “ROTC Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Prohibition of closures of Senior ROTC programs of the Army

During the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on July 1, 2020, the Secretary of the Army may not, pursuant to Department of Defense Instruction 1215.08, disestablish, or initiate the disestablishment of, a Senior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps program of the Army established pursuant to chapter 103 of title 10, United States Code.
