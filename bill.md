# S.J. RES. 31

> Relating to the approval of the proposed Agreement for Cooperation Between the American Institute in Taiwan and the Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office in the United States Concerning Peaceful Uses of Nuclear Energy.

That the Congress does favor the proposed agreement for cooperation transmitted to the Congress by the President on January 7, 2014.
