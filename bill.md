# H.R. 4393

> To prohibit any Federal agency or official, in carrying out any Act or program to reduce the effects of greenhouse gas emissions on climate change, from imposing a fee or tax on gaseous emissions emitted directly by livestock.

## § 1. Prohibition of imposition of fee or tax on gaseous emissions by livestock

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, in carrying out any Act or program to reduce the effects of greenhouse gas emissions on climate change, no Federal agency or official shall impose a fee or tax on gaseous emissions emitted directly by livestock.
