# H.R. 1400

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to clarify that children of certain veterans are eligible for the Marine Gunnery Sergeant John David Fry scholarship.

## § 1. Clarification of Marine Gunnery Sergeant John David Fry Scholarship

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3311(b)(9) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “in line of duty” and all that follows through the period and inserting “as a result of a service-connected disability.”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if included in the enactment of section 1002 of the Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2009 (Public Law 111–32; 123 Stat. 1889).
