# H.R. 5731

> To extend foreclosure and eviction protections for servicemembers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Servicemembers Foreclosure Protection Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Extension of protections for members of uniformed services relating to mortgages, mortgage foreclosure, and eviction

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 710(d) of the Honoring America’s Veterans and Caring for Camp Lejeune Families Act of 2012 (Public Law 112–154; 126 Stat. 1208) is amended by striking “2014” and inserting “2016”.
- **(b)** *Repeal of revival provision* Section 710(d) of the Honoring America’s Veterans and Caring for Camp Lejeune Families Act of 2012 (Public Law 112–154; 126 Stat. 1208) is amended by striking paragraph (3).
