# H.R. 5008

> To prohibit United States voluntary contributions to the United Nations Democracy Fund.

## § 1. Prohibition on United States voluntary contributions to the United Nations Democracy Fund

- **(a)** *In general* No funds made available to any Federal department or agency may be used to make United States voluntary contributions to the United Nations Democracy Fund (UNDEF).
- **(b)** *Effective date* Subsection (a) applies with respect to funds made available for fiscal year 2015 and subsequent fiscal years.
