# H.R. 2866

That the bill from the House of Representatives (H.R. 2866) entitled “An Act to require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in commemoration of the centennial of Boys Town, and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*On page 7, strike lines 8 through 10, and insert the following: “shall be paid to the United States Treasury for the purposes of reducing the national debt.”.*
