# S. 2362

> To prohibit the payment of performance awards in fiscal year 2015 to employees in the Veterans Health Administration, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Prohibition on payment of performance awards to employees in Veterans Health Administration

Notwithstanding section 5384 of title 5, United States Code, in fiscal year 2015, no performance award shall be paid under such section to employees in the Veterans Health Administration.
