# H.R. 3973

> To amend section 530D of title 28, United States Code.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Faithful Execution of the Law Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Amendment to section 530D of title 28, United States Code

Section 530D(a)(1)(A) of title 28, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “or any other Federal officer” before “establishes or implements a formal or informal policy”; and
- **(2)** in clause (i), by striking “on the grounds that such provision is unconstitutional” and inserting “and state the grounds for such policy”.
