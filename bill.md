# H.R. 4661

> To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2015 for intelligence and intelligence-related activities of the United States Government, the Community Management Account, and the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2015”.
