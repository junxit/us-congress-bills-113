# H.R. 3547

> To extend the application of certain space launch liability provisions through 2014.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Space Launch Liability Indemnification Extension Act”.

## § 2. Indemnification extension

Section 50915(f) of title 51, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2013” and inserting “December 31, 2014”.
