# H.R. 5175

> To amend the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to repeal the risk corridor program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Americans from Illegal Bailouts Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Repeal of the risk corridor program established in PPACA

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1342 of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 18062) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as of the date of the enactment of this Act.
