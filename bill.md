# H.R. 1567

> To eliminate corporate welfare programs of the Department of Agriculture, the Department of the Interior, the Department of Transportation, and other Federal agencies.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “New Fair Deal Consolidating and Eliminating Outdated Subsidies Act of 2013” or “New Fair Deal CEO’s Act of 2013”.
