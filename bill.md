# H.R. 413

> To eliminate the 2-year delay in including oral-only ESRD-related drugs in the Medicare ESRD prospective payment system, as provided under section 632(b)(1) of the American Taxpayer Relief Act of 2012.

## § 1. Eliminating the 2-year delay in implementation of oral-only ESRD-related drugs in the Medicare ESRD prospective payment system

Section 632(b) of the American Taxpayer Relief Act of 2012 (Public Law 112–240) is amended by striking all that precedes “With respect to the implementation” and inserting the following::

> - **(b)** *Monitoring of implementation of oral-Only ESRD-Related drugs in the ESRD prospective payment system* With respect to the implementation
