# S. 1287

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to raise the limitation on the election to accelerate the AMT credit in lieu of bonus depreciation for 2013.

## § 1. Temporary increase in limitation on election to accelerate the AMT credit in lieu of bonus depreciation

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (J) of section 168(k)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new clause:
  > - **(v)** *Special maximum increase amount for 2013* In the case of round 3 extension property placed in service by a corporation—
  >   - **(I)** subparagraph (C)(iii) shall not apply, and
  >   - **(II)** the term maximum increase amount means an amount that is 50 percent of the AMT credit increase amount determined with respect to such corporation under subparagraph (E).

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2012, in taxable years ending after such date.
