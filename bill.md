# H.R. 3802

> To extend the legislative authority of the Adams Memorial Foundation to establish a commemorative work in honor of former President John Adams and his legacy, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension of legislative authority for memorial establishment

Section 1(c) of Public Law 107–62 (40 U.S.C. 1003 note), as amended by Public Law 111–169, is amended by striking “2013” and inserting “2020”.
