# H.R. 2478

> To repeal a limitation on Federal procurement of certain fuels.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Section 526 Repeal Act”.

## § 2. Repeal

Section 526 of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 17142) is repealed.
