# S. 2760

> To extend National Highway Traffic Safety Administration authorizations, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Motor Vehicle and Highway Safety Enhancement Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Definition of Secretary

In this Act, unless expressly provided otherwise, the term Secretary means the Secretary of Transportation.
