# H.R. 5816

> To extend the authorization for the United States Commission on International Religious Freedom.

## § 1. Extension and termination of authority

The International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 is amended—

- **(1)** in section 207(a) (22 U.S.C. 6435(a)), by striking “2014” and inserting “2015”; and
- **(2)** in section 209 (22 U.S.C. 6436), by striking “September 30, 2014” and inserting “September 30, 2015”.

## § 2. Effective date

The amendments made by this Act shall take effect as if enacted on December 10, 2014.
