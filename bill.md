# S. 515

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the Yellow Ribbon G.I. Education Enhancement Program to cover recipients of Marine Gunnery Sergeant John David Fry scholarship, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Expansion of Yellow Ribbon G.I. Education Enhancement Program

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3317(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “in paragraphs (1) and (2)” and inserting “in paragraphs (1), (2), and (9)”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to academic years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
