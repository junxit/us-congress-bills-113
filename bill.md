# S. 1929

> To require the Secretary of the Interior to transfer to the State of Alaska certain land for the purpose of building a road between the community of King Cove and the all-weather airport in Cold Bay, Alaska.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “King Cove All-Weather Road Corridor Act”.
