# H.R. 4218

> To reauthorize the Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area

Section 7 of the Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 461 note; Public Law 106–319; 114 Stat. 1284) is amended by striking “2015” and inserting “2030”.
