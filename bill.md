# H.R. 5718

> To study the effect of the Earth’s magnetic field on the weather.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stockman Effect Act”.

## § 2. Magnetic field study

- **(a)** *Findings* Congress finds as follows:
  - **(1)** Prior to a magnetic polar shift, there is a decline in the Earth’s magnetic fields.
  - **(2)** Decrease in magnetic fields could impact global temperatures.
  - **(3)** There is a possibility that the reason Mars lost its atmosphere was because of the loss of its magnetic field.
- **(b)** *Magnetic field study* The director of the National Science Foundation shall commission a study on the impact that a shift in the Earth’s magnetic field could have on the weather.
