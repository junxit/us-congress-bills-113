# H.R. 2819

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 275 Front Street in Marietta, Ohio, as the Veterans Memorial Post Office Building.

## § 1. Veterans Memorial Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 275 Front Street in Marietta, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the Veterans Memorial Post Office Building.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Veterans Memorial Post Office Building.
