# S. 1826

> To provide predictability and certainty in the tax law, create jobs, and encourage investment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Motorsports Fairness and Permanency Act”.

## § 2. 7-year class life for motorsports entertainment complex facilities made permanent

Section 168(i)(15) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subparagraph (D).
