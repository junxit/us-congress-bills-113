# H.R. 3590

> To protect and enhance opportunities for recreational hunting, fishing, and shooting, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Sportsmen’s Heritage And Recreational Enhancement Act of 2013” or the “SHARE Act of 2013”.
