# H.R. 5568

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to direct the Secretary of Education to award interest-free student loans to certain students, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “B-On-Time Student Loan Relief Act of 2014”.
