# H.R. 3956

> To amend the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 to authorize the Small Business Administrator to make grants for economic growth, business retention and business recruitment to economically underserved communities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Investment and Empowerment Act”.

## § 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to assist with the economic growth of economically disadvantaged communities that have potential for strong Class 1 commercial investment, but continue to have a difficult time recruiting Class 1 commercial investment.
