# H.R. 3053

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the authority of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to carry out the Department of Veterans Affairs Health Professional Scholarship Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Healthcare for our Heroes Act”.

## § 2. Extension of Department of Veterans Affairs Health Professional Scholarship Program

Section 7619 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2014” and inserting “December 31, 2016”.
