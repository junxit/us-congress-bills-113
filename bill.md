# H.R. 2251

> To designate the United States courthouse and Federal building located at 118 South Mill Street, in Fergus Falls, Minnesota, as the Edward J. Devitt United States Courthouse and Federal Building.

## § 1. Designation

The United States courthouse and Federal building located at 118 South Mill Street, in Fergus Falls, Minnesota, shall be known and designated as the Edward J. Devitt United States Courthouse and Federal Building.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States courthouse and Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the Edward J. Devitt United States Courthouse and Federal Building.
