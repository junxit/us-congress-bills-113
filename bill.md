# H. RES. 741

> Disapproving of the President’s expression of intent to expand amnesty to undocumented immigrants through Executive order after the 2014 congressional midterm elections.

That the House of Representatives disapproves of the President’s expression of intent to expand amnesty to undocumented immigrants through Executive order after the 2014 congressional midterm elections.
