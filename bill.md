# S. 2962

> To repeal the tax increase imposed by Obamacare on taxpayers who incur catastrophic medical expenses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medical Expense Deduction Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of increase in minimum deduction for medical, dental, etc., expenses

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (a) of section 213 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “10 percent” and inserting “7.5 percent”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2014.
