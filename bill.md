# S. 2121

> To repeal title II of the REAL ID Act of 2005.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repeal REAL ID Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of title II of the REAL ID Act of 2005

Title II of the REAL ID Act of 2005 (division B of Public Law 109–13; 49 U.S.C. 30301 note) is repealed.
