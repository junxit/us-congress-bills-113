# H.R. 1495

> To prohibit the further extension or establishment of national monuments in Arizona except by express authorization of Congress.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Arizona Land Sovereignty Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on further extension or establishment of national monuments in Arizona

The proviso of the last sentence of the first section of the Act of September 14, 1950 (64 Stat. 849, chapter 950; 16 U.S.C. 431a), is amended by inserting “or Arizona” after “Wyoming”.
