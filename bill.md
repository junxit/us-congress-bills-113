# H.R. 3282

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to reauthorize the predisaster hazard mitigation program.

## § 1. Predisaster hazard mitigation program

Section 203(m) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5133(m)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(m)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $200,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2014 through 2018.
