# H.R. 5508

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to eliminate the marriage penalty in the dollar limitation on the student loan interest deduction.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Student Loan Interest Deduction Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Elimination of marriage penalty in dollar limitation on the student loan interest deduction

- **(a)** *In general* Section 221(b)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “shall not exceed” and all that follows and inserting “shall not exceed $2,500 (twice such amount in the case of a joint return)”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
