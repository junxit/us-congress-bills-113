# H.R. 2007

> To eliminate the limitation on the period for which borrowers are eligible for guaranteed assistance under the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act.

## § 1. Elimination of limitation on period borrowers are eligible for guaranteed assistance

Section 319 of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1949) is amended by striking subsection (b).
