# H.R. 4651

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 601 West Baker Road in Baytown, Texas, as the “Specialist Keith Erin Grace, Jr. Memorial Post Office”.

## § 1. Specialist Keith Erin Grace, Jr. Memorial Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 601 West Baker Road in Baytown, Texas, shall be known as the “Specialist Keith Erin Grace, Jr. Memorial Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referenced to subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Specialist Keith Erin Grace, Jr. Memorial Post Office”.
