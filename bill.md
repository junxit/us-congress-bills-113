# H.R. 3409

> To amend the National Wildlife Refuge System Administration Act of 1966 to require that any expansion of a national wildlife refuge must be expressly authorized by statute.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Wildlife Refuge Expansion Limitation Act of 2013”.

## § 2. Prohibition on expansion of national wildlife refuges

Section 4(a) of the National Wildlife Refuge System Administration Act of 1966 (16 U.S.C. 668dd(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(6)** The Secretary may not expand any national wildlife refuge except as expressly authorized by a law enacted after January 3, 2013.
