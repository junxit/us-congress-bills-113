# H.R. 4421

> To extend the authorization for the Automobile National Heritage Area in Michigan.

## § 1. Automobile National Heritage Area Authorization extended

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “MotorCities National Heritage Area Extension Act”.
- **(b)** *Authorization* Section 109 of the Automobile National Heritage Area Act (16 U.S.C. 461 note; Public Law 105–355) is amended by striking “September 30, 2014” and inserting “September 30, 2030”.
