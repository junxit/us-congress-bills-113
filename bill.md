# S. 2789

> To amend part B of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act to provide full Federal funding of such part.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “IDEA Full Funding Act”.

## § 2. Amendments to idea

Section 611(i) of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1411(i)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(i)** *Funding* For the purpose of carrying out this part, other than section 619, there are authorized to be appropriated—
>   - **(1)** $12,955,841,000 for fiscal year 2015, and there are hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, $1,377,986,000 for fiscal year 2015, which shall become available for obligation on July 1, 2015, and shall remain available through September 30, 2016;
