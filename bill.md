# H.R. 4845

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the deduction for mortgage insurance premiums.

## § 1. Permanent deduction for mortgage insurance premiums

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (E) of section 163(h)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking clause (iv).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or accrued after December 31, 2013, and to amounts properly allocable to periods after such date.
