# H.R. 4492

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide for the availability of breastfeeding support, supplies, and counseling under the TRICARE program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “TRICARE Moms Improvement Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Availability of breastfeeding support, supplies, and counseling under the tricare program

Section 1079(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(18)** Breastfeeding support, supplies (including breast pumps and associated equipment), and counseling shall be provided as appropriate during pregnancy and the postpartum period.
