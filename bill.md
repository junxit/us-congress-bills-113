# H.R. 726

> To amend the Native American Programs Act of 1974 to reauthorize a provision to ensure the survival and continuing vitality of Native American languages.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Native American languages program

Section 816(e) of the Native American Programs Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 2992d(e)) is amended by striking “2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012” and inserting “2013 through 2017”.
