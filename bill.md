# S. 2979

> To extend eligibility for hospital care, medical services, and nursing home and domiciliary care for certain veterans who served in a theater of combat operations.

## § 1. Extension of eligibility for hospital care, medical services, and nursing home and domiciliary care for certain veterans who served in a theater of combat operations

Section 1710(e)(3)(A) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “period of five years” and inserting “period of 10 years”.
