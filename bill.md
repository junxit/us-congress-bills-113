# H.R. 5856

> To prohibit the United States Department of Homeland Security from purchasing, operating, or maintaining armed unmanned aerial vehicles.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Armed Drones for DHS Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on purchase, use, or maintenance of armed drones

The United States Department of Homeland Security is prohibited from purchasing, operating, or maintaining armed unmanned aerial vehicles.
