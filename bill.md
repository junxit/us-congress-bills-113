# S. 1077

> To amend the Chesapeake Bay Initiative Act of 1998 to provide for the reauthorization of the Chesapeake Bay Gateways and Watertrails Network.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Chesapeake Bay Gateways and Watertrails Network Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

Section 502(c) of the Chesapeake Bay Initiative Act of 1998 (16 U.S.C. 461 note; Public Law 105–312) is amended by striking “fiscal years” and all that follows through the period at the end and inserting “fiscal years 2014 through 2018.”.
