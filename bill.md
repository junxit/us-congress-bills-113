# H.R. 3176

> To reauthorize the Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief Act of 1991, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension of Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief Act of 1991

- **(a)** *Termination of authority* Section 104(c) of the Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief Act of 1991 (43 U.S.C. 2214(c)) is amended by striking “2012” and inserting “2018”.
- **(b)** *Authorization of Appropriations* Section 301 of the Reclamation States Emergency Drought Relief Act of 1991 (43 U.S.C. 2241) is amended by striking “2012” and inserting “2018”.
