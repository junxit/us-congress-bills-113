# H.R. 2391

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 5323 Highway N in Cottleville, Missouri as the Lance Corporal Phillip Vinnedge Post Office.

## § 1. Lance Corporal Phillip D. Vinnedge Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 5323 Highway N in Cottleville, Missouri, shall be known and designated as the Lance Corporal Phillip Vinnedge Post Office.
- **(b)** *References* Any references in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Lance Corporal Phillip Vinnedge Post Office.
