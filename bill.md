# H.R. 5871

> To tax capital gains at the same rate as ordinary income.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Dollar is a Dollar Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of capital gains tax cap

Subsection (h), entitled “Maximum capital gains rate”, of section 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is hereby repealed.
