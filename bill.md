# H.R. 1590

> To amend the Specialty Crops Competitiveness Act of 2004 to include farmed shellfish as specialty crops.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Shellfish Marketing Assistance Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Farmed shellfish as specialty crops

Section 3(1) of the Specialty Crops Competitiveness Act of 2004 (7 U.S.C. 1621 note; Public Law 108–465) is amended by inserting “farmed shellfish, ” after “fruits,”.
