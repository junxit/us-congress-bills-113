# S.J. RES. 30

> To disapprove a rule of the Environmental Protection Agency relating to greenhouse gas emissions from electric utility generating units.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to new source performance standards for emissions of carbon dioxide for new affected fossil fuel-fired electric utility generating units (published at 79 Fed. Reg. 1430 (January 8, 2014)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
