# H.R. 5466

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4500 Sunny Isle Shopping Center in Christiansted, St. Croix, United States Virgin Islands, as the “Florence Louise Thomas Post Office”.

## § 1. Florence Louise Thomas Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4500 Sunny Isle Shopping Center in Christiansted, St. Croix, United States Virgin Islands, shall be known and designated as the “Florence Louise Thomas Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Florence Louise Thomas Post Office”.
