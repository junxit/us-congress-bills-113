# H.R. 3944

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year tax-free distributions from individual retirement plans for charitable purposes.

## § 1. Extension of tax-free distributions from individual retirement plans for charitable purposes

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (F) of section 408(d)(8) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2013” and inserting “December 31, 2014”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to distributions made in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2013.
