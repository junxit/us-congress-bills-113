# H.R. 4120

> To amend the National Law Enforcement Museum Act to extend the termination date.

## § 1. National Law Enforcement Museum Act termination date extended

Section 4(f) of the National Law Enforcement Museum Act (Public Law 106–492) is amended by striking “13 years” and inserting “16 years”.

## § 2. Effective date

The provisions of this Act shall take effect as if this Act were enacted on November 8, 2013.
