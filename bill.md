# H.R. 2014

> To repeal section 2703(c)(2)(C) of title 18, United States Code.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Telephone Records Protection Act”.

## § 2. Repeal

Subparagraph (C) of section 2703(c)(2) of title 18, United States Code, is repealed.
