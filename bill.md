# H.R. 4340

> To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to passenger motor vehicle crash avoidance information, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safety Through Informed Consumers Act of 2014”.

## § 2. Passenger motor vehicle information

Section 32302 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by inserting after subsection (b) the following:

> - **(c)** *Crash avoidance* Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of the Safety Through Informed Consumers Act of 2014, the Secretary shall ensure that crash avoidance information is provided alongside of and in the same format as crashworthiness information each place that crashworthiness information is made available to the public under this section.
