# H.R. 4911

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Glendale, New York.

## § 1. Single, unique ZIP Code for Glendale, New York

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this section, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code applicable to the area encompassing only Glendale, New York.
