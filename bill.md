# H.R. 2039

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to furnish headstones and markers for certain deceased veterans buried in veterans’ cemeteries of Indian tribes.

## § 1. Headstones and markers for placement in tribal veterans’ cemeteries

Section 2306 of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(4), by inserting “or in a veterans’ cemetery on land owned by or held in trust for a tribal organization (as defined in section 3765(4) of this title)” after “State”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b)(1), by inserting “a veterans’ cemetery on land owned by or held in trust for a tribal organization,” after “a veterans’ cemetery owned by a State,”.
