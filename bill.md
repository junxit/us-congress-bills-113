# H.R. 3843

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income discharges of Federal student loans as a result of veterans’ service-connected total disability that is permanent in nature.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veteran Tax Parity Act”.

## § 2. Exclusion from gross income for discharge of Federal student loans of veterans as a result of permanent service-connected total disability

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 108(f) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “or if such discharge resulted from an individual having a service-connected total disability that is permanent in nature as determined by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs” before the period at the end.
