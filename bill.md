# H.R. 1198

> To provide for American Samoa and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands to be treated as States for certain criminal justice programs.

## § 1. Treatment as a State for American Samoa and the CNMI

Section 901(a)(2) of Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3791(a)(2)) is amended by striking “Islands:” and all that follows through the period and inserting “Islands;”.
