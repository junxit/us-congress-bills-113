# H.R. 3759

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the employer wage credit for activated military reservists.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Military Reserve Small Business Jobs Act of 2013”.

## § 2. Extension of employer wage credit for activated military reservists

- **(a)** *In general* Section 45P(f) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2013” and inserting “December 31, 2018”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to payments made after December 31, 2013.
