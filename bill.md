# H.R. 439

> To ensure that the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority complies with certain Federal regulations and statutes.

## § 1. Application of Federal regulations and statutes

- **(a)** *Federal acquisition regulation* The Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority shall comply with the Federal Acquisition Regulation.
- **(b)** *Employment of relatives; restrictions* Section 3110(a)(1) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (C) by striking “and”;
  - **(2)** by redesignating subparagraph (D) as subparagraph (E); and
  - **(3)** by inserting after subparagraph (C) the following:
    > - **(D)** the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority; and
