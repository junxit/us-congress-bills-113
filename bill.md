# H.R. 5570

> To reauthorize the Historically Black Colleges and Universities Historic Preservation program.

## § 1. Historically Black Colleges and Universities Historic Preservation program reauthorized

Section 507(d)(2) of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (16 U.S.C. 470a note) is amended by striking the period at the end and inserting “and each of fiscal years 2015 through 2024”.
