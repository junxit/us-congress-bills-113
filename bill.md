# S. 2830

> To permanently reauthorize the special diabetes programs for Indians.

## § 1. Special diabetes programs for Indians

Section 330C(c)(2)(C) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–3(c)(2)(C)) is amended by striking “each of fiscal years 2004 through 2015” and inserting “fiscal year 2004 and each fiscal year thereafter”.
