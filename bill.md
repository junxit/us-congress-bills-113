# S. 1578

> To authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to cover the costs associated with the care of veterans at medical foster homes.

## § 1. Coverage of costs of care for veterans at medical foster homes

In conducting the medical foster home program pursuant to section 17.73 of title 38, Code of Federal Regulations, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs may cover the costs associated with the care of veterans at medical foster homes.
