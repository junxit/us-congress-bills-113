# S. 2936

> To provide that in the case of leases to local education agencies and elementary and secondary schools, consideration may be at or below fair market value or for no consideration.

## § 1. Leasing of non-excess property of military departments and Defense Agencies; treatment of value provided by local education agencies and elementary and secondary schools

Section 2667 of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:

> - **(k)** *Leases for education* In the case of a lease under this section to a local education agency or an elementary or secondary school (as those terms are defined in section 9101 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7801)), consideration may be at or below fair market value or for no consideration.
