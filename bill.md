# H.R. 5114

> To facilitate the expedited processing of minors entering the United States across the southern border and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Helping Unaccompanied Minors and Alleviating National Emergency Act” or the “HUMANE Act”.
