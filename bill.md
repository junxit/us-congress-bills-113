# S. 1866

> To provide for an extension of the legislative authority of the Adams Memorial Foundation to establish a commemorative work in honor of former President John Adams and his legacy.

## § 1. Extension of legislative authority for memorial establishment

Section 1(c) of Public Law 107–62, as amended by Public Law 111–169, is amended by striking “2013” and inserting “2020”.
