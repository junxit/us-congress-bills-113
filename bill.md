# H.R. 4702

> To extend until 2030 the time period required to be covered by the United States Space Protection Strategy.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Space Protection Strategy Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of time period to be covered by the space protection strategy

Section 911(d) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2008 (10 U.S.C. 2271 note) is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(4)** Fiscal years 2026 through 2030.
